Where do I get a JSON file?

For one chat, open its menu in Telegram Desktop and choose Export chat history.

For several chats, open Settings → Advanced → Export Telegram data and select the chat types you want.

Choose Machine-readable JSON and open result.json here. You can leave media unchecked.
